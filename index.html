How to play

Each level is built around one pattern found in nature. You'll go through five short stages:

  1. 🎨 Sketch — trace the pattern's outline on a canvas.
  2. 📐 Geometry — answer two math/geometry questions about the pattern you just traced.
  3. 🔤 Spell — drag letter tiles (Scrabble-style, with point values) to spell related vocabulary.
  4. ♻️ Choose sustainably — pick the more sustainable material for an everyday item, and see the real trade-offs.
  5. 🎲 Nature's Luck — pick one of six face-down cards for a bonus; all six flip over afterward so you can see the real odds, framed around a genuine chance event in nature (seed survival, pollination, ocean dispersal, or prey capture).

Sources & honesty notes

  • Honeycomb Conjecture / Theorem, proved by Thomas Hales (1999) — summarized via ResearchGate and arXiv.
  • Leaf venation patterns — Biology Insights; Digital Atlas of Ancient Life.
  • Nautilus shell logarithmic spiral & the golden-ratio myth — Science Friday ("Myth or math? Is the nautilus shell's golden ratio real?").
  • Spider orb-web radial geometry — European Arachnology Society; Aptive Pest Control.
  • Plastic / plastic bag decomposition timelines — chariotenergy.com; decodingbiosphere.com.
  • Beeswax wrap vs. plastic wrap — UC ANR Preservation Notes; The Green Collective.
  • Stainless-steel vs. single-use plastic bottles — myequa.com; icobottle.com.
  • Hemp vs. cotton vs. polyester water use & biodegradability — hemp.com; ecothes.com; Slate.
  • Oak acorn/seedling overwintering survival — USDA Forest Service (research.fs.usda.gov).
  • Honey bee flower-visiting behavior — pollinatorparadise.com.
  • Marine larval mortality & dispersal research summaries.
  • Orb-weaver prey interception & capture-efficiency research.

Honesty note: the sketch shapes are stylized teaching guides, not botanically exact drawings. Material "sustainability scores" are simplified teaching ranks based on the sources above, not precise lifecycle-assessment numbers — real sustainability trade-offs depend on how often something is reused, how it's disposed of, and local conditions. The Nature's Luck odds (how many of the 6 cards hide a bonus) are simplified, playable numbers loosely inspired by the cited research, not the literal cited percentages — real survival/capture odds vary enormously by species and conditions, which is itself the point: nature really is this unpredictable.

Privacy policy — this game collects no personal information.

Accessibility

  • Every color cue is paired with an icon, label, or pattern (never color alone).
  • The sketch stage has a one-click "Skip (accessibility)" option that awards a fair baseline score — mouse/touch drawing is not required to progress.
  • All interactive elements are real buttons, reachable and triggerable by keyboard (Tab + Enter/Space).
  • Feedback text uses aria-live so screen readers announce results as they appear.
